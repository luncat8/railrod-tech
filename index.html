PHASE 01 / OPTIMUM EMERGENCE

The train never
turns back.

A seeded ring of fourteen nodes wraps 64 km of track. The train runs one way round it and stops only where it can trade: unloading into a consumer that needs its cargo, or loading from a source at a margin. Money is not limited, so the number to watch is NET/S — trade profit per second less the build's capex, amortised. The panel top right re-runs the loop headlessly over a 9 × 9 × 3 grid of gauge, wheel and engine: every dot is a build measured in the sim, the crosshair is yours. Slide a knob and see how far off the measured optimum you sit. Tick LINE and the panel becomes a bench instead: a fixed length of track, totalled rather than averaged, run by as many trains as you like on the same world.
