PHASE 01 / ONE-WAY LOOP

The train never
turns back.

A seeded ring of fourteen nodes wraps 64 km of track. The train runs one way round it and stops only where it can trade: unloading into a consumer that needs its cargo, or loading from a source at a margin. Money is not limited, so the number to watch is NET/S — trade profit per second less the build's capex, amortised. Change the gauge, wheel, engine and wagons and watch it move.
