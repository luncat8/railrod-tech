# 0.1.4 — loop trade, profit rate, reconfigurable build worklog

## Scope change from the request

- The 0.1.3 shuttle and trip estimate are retired. The train runs one way round
  the loop, in the direction of increasing x, and stops only where a trade pays.
- The trading and profit design moved to `deferred-plan.md`. 0.1.4 keeps the parts
  the stop rule and profit rate need: the trade rules, the profit EMA, and capex.
- Click-to-route and waypoint marks are removed.
- The build is reconfigurable live: wagons, gauge (0.6–4.0 m readout), wheel
  (0.4–1.6 m), engine (20–60 t). Capex is an amortised rate, so the rate telemetry
  moves when a knob moves.

## Implementation

- `js/const.js`: knob ranges, `BRAKE_DECEL` 0.6, `UNITS_PER_S` 2, `STOP_EPS_KM`
  0.01, `PROFIT_TAU_S` 60, `AMORT_S` 1200 (provisional), `K_TRACK`, `CW`, `CL`,
  the readout ranges. `DWELL_S` and the profile constants are removed.
- `js/tech.js` (new): knob → build (`derive`), `clampKnob`, `capexRate`,
  `gaugeM`, `wheelM`. The formulas are the 0.1.3 ones, plus the cost terms.
- `js/trade.js` (new): `wantsUnload`, `bestSell`, `loadMargin`, `pickLoad`,
  `wantsStop`, `transact`. A stop unloads first, then loads, one unit at a time,
  each quoted after the previous unit has moved stock.
- `js/train.js`: one-way loop. `scanStop` finds the nearest wanted node ahead;
  `train.last` is the node just departed and is ignored until the train is
  `STOP_EPS_KM` clear of it. Braking is `max(BRAKE_DECEL, v²/2d)` above the cap
  `min(vTrack, sqrt(2·B·d))`. Arrival captures the train on the node and dwells
  for `moved / UNITS_PER_S`. `setWagons` keeps the loaded floor. `setBuild`
  refreshes capex.
- `js/sim.js`: knobs, `setKnob`, `setWagons`, the profit EMA over 60 s, and
  `netRate = profitRate − capexRate`.
- `js/economy.js`: `world.produced` ledger in `tickSource`.
- `js/world.js`: `produced`, `consumed`, `overflow` and `stock` are float64.
- `js/render.js`: consist with cargo fills, dwell bar over `dwellTotal`, no
  waypoint marks.
- `js/main.js`, `js/ui.js`, `index.html`: click routing removed; three knob
  sliders with physical readouts; NET, PROFIT, CAPEX and CARGO telemetry; TRIP
  removed; badge `PROTOTYPE 0.1.4`; next-step card `0.1.5`.

## Bugs found and fixed while testing

- **Stops were skipped.** A distance-only skip of wanted nodes within 0.01 km
  dropped the node the train was creeping up to, so the train passed it. Runs
  showed zero stops. The skip now covers only the node just departed
  (`train.last`).
- **Constant-decel braking overshot.** `sqrt(2·B·d)` falls faster than `B` once
  the train is above it, so a constant brake always ends short of the stop. The
  brake is now `max(B, v²/2d)` above the cap. A late stop gets a harder brake.
- **The profit EMA was too short.** At τ = 10 s the readout was mostly ripple
  (std 2.19 cr/s against a mean of 1.4). At 60 s, about one loop, std was 0.52.
- **Cargo was all R1 at creation.** `Train.create` zero-filled the `Int8Array`,
  and 0 is R1. `create` now fills with −1.
- **The mass balance drifted.** A float32 ledger drifted about 0.003 units/s
  (0.01 after 40 s of trade). Stock and the ledger are now float64; the residual is
  about 1e-11.

## Decisions

- Tuning is deferred to 0.1.5. The stage parent and the 0.1.4 plan both say so,
  and the sweep is a 0.1.5 non-goal. `AMORT_S`, `BRAKE_DECEL`, `UNITS_PER_S`, and
  the cost constants are provisional.
- Cash is unbounded and capex is an amortised rate, `build value / AMORT_S`.
  The efficiency measure is NET/s.
- Sources do not buy back. Consumers refuse a unit unless they need it and have
  room for it.
- The loop runs in the direction of increasing x. This is the working reading of
  "right"; the user has not yet confirmed it.

## Next step

Implement **0.1.5 — optimum emergence**. Write `experiments/surface.js`: a
headless sweep of the 9×9×3 `(g, d, e)` grid over at least five seeds, measuring
`netRate` in the sim (300 s warm-up, 900 s measured, as in `tau.js`). Check that on
at least 3 of 5 seeds the surface has one interior maximum, that the enormous
engine on 4 m gauge is worse than the optimum on every seed, and that a knob change
mid-run never jumps prices or stock. Then tune `AMORT_S` and the cost constants
until the optimum is interior, and add the performance panel. The 0.1.4 default
build's NET of +0.25 cr/s is the baseline to beat.
