## Implementation

- `js/const.js`: the force-model table (knob defaults `g = d = e = 0.5`, `ML`,
  `M0`/`MW`, `V0`, `P0`, `C_RR0`, `C_DRAG`, `MU`, `DRIVE_SHARE`, the scaled
  `TRAIN_G`, `UNIT_T`, `V_EPS`, the wagon range, `DWELL_S`, and the profile
  integration step/cap). The `TRAIN_V` placeholder is gone.
- `js/train.js` (new): `derive` turns the knob triple into a build; `accel` is
  the signed force model (`min(F_trac, P/max(v,V_EPS)) - c_rr·M·G - c_drag·v³`,
  divided by `M`); `advance` is the pure kinematics step (returns km covered,
  wraps `x`); `refreshProfile` integrates the accel ODE once per build change
  into `{T, S, v}`; `estimateTrip` is the closed form over that profile;
  `step` is the WAIT / CRUISE / DWELL state machine with the A↔B shuttle, a
  sub-step arrival capture, and `setDestination` / `clearRoute` / `setWagons`.
- `js/sim.js`: `sim.trains` is an array (length 1 — the shape the 0.2
  competitor trains need); `Sim.step` ticks the economy and then every train.
- `js/render.js`: the consist (loco + `W` hollow wagon frames, mirrored by
  direction, centred on the train), the dwell progress bar, and waypoint marks
  under the two ends of the shuttle line.
- `js/main.js` + `js/ui.js` + `index.html` + `css/app.css`: canvas click routes
  the train, a `WAGONS` range control in the footer, and two new telemetry
  items — train speed and `TRIP measured / planned` — so the estimate check is
  readable in the page. The reported trip is frozen at arrival
  (`lastTripTime`), not read from the running clock.
- `experiments/train.js` (new): mass model, profile reaches the cap, wagon
  sweep (monotone, ×1.37 for a doubling), estimate accuracy (worst 2.53%),
  arrival/dwell/shuttle, park-and-route, retargeting while cruising, the
  antipodal tie, seam-crossing legs, the speed cap and sign bounds, a stalled
  build, determinism, and a long run on a seeded world.
- `experiments/world.js`: the seam and camera tests drive `Train.advance`
  instead of the removed constant-speed placeholder.
- `experiments/harness.js`: the 1× and 8× schedules now also compare every
  train field and a world stock checksum.
- `experiments/boot.js`: exercises the wagons control (input event and direct),
  click routing (hover finds node 1's column, the click sends the train there,
  the finished trip is within 10% of the plan), and parking.
- `README.md`, `findings-pitfalls-skills.md`, and the `index.html` copy, badge,
  capabilities, and next-step card updated to 0.1.3 → 0.1.4.
