  RNG and parks the train at node 0.

  is invisible; pure `screenX` / `cameraStep` / `copyOffset` helpers exported
  for node tests.
- `js/main.js`: passes the sim and the clamped frame dt to draw; snaps the
  camera after a seed change.
- `index.html`: `world.js` added to the dep order; badge 0.1.1; intro copy,
  capabilities, and next-step point at 0.1.2.
- `experiments/world.js`: 100-seed spacing, determinism, world shape, seam
